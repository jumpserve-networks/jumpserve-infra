# Review-phase correction after production positive control, v8

The first release passed its declared fourteen development cases, then the real
authenticated IPv6 positive control exposed a coverage gap in those cases: some
comprehension calls selected another check instead of returning a review. The
controller rejected those actions without conclusions or launches but paused
claims instead of issuing the existing invalid-output correction path. Preserve
the v5 publication, every original prompt/answer, context-size blockers, external
data blockers and the interrupted six-invocation operator campaign. Do not call
the initial rollout fully verified. Temporarily hold autonomous admission and
the minute schedule during correction; other modules and all records remain.

Bind each request's tool action enum to its actual controller phase and add a
server-authored phase reminder after the unchanged untrusted context. Independently
validate the returned action against that phase before marking the turn complete.
A confirmed invalid answer stays retained as invalid-output and receives a new
numbered prompt with protocol-error feedback, without prior review verdicts.
Unknown provider outcomes remain blocked from automatic replay. Readiness and
coverage gates, original claims and conditions, model, paper defaults, context
bound, budgets and cost-envelope denial are unchanged. This correction does not
make an invalid review ready or weaken a scientific criterion.

Before evaluation, freeze v6 implementation/request locks and a new sixteen-case
development manifest. Preserve all fourteen original inputs and criteria byte for
byte as entries, adding two explicit synthetic positive controls: comprehension
and critic of a complete single matched-cell software fixture. They must return
review, ready=true and coverage_complete=true for that fixture's narrow scope;
they are not scientific paper results. Review all sixteen actual raw and rendered
answers, including specific questions, before publication. No cherry-picking or
independent/held-out/human validation claim.

One full corrective candidate campaign is permitted: sixteen new calls, at least
15 seconds between calls, no SDK/CLI retry, existing call/wall/context/output limits,
at most USD8 conservative commitments for this campaign and USD40 across the six
candidate campaigns plus the prior five provider-only recoveries. This permits
91 evaluation calls total. Positive-control application usage is separately
recorded under each paper's unchanged limits, not hidden in evaluation costs.
Stop publication after any failed answer or provider call. Add publication guards
and a new prompt version rather than modifying earlier migration or result bytes.

After corrected publication and exact-code deployment, remove the admission hold,
verify the schedule and anonymous boundary again, and submit a new named private
positive control through the real form. Retain the first study with its original
outcomes. Verify a new executed retained-data run and distinguish its assessments
from historical assessments imported by preparation. In total, allow at most two
private positive-control papers for this release. No new cloud networking.
