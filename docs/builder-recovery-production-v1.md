This is a historical reproduction procedure, not authorization to repeat a
model campaign or apply a historical source approval to changed code. A new
candidate needs a newly frozen protocol and exact-source qualification. Never
rerun a dispatched live phase; inspect retained receipts first. The selected
campaign and raw evidence stay in `.test-artifacts/builder-recovery-release-v1`.
Qualification and deployment archives are private, with per-file manifests and
GET-after-upload byte verification. Failed read-only target requests are retained
separately and can safely be retried; uncertain model or execution calls cannot.

The read-only `snapshot` mode verifies original parent identities against 102
retained records, retrieves proof/checkpoint bytes, checks native call coverage,
and confirms the $1,000 assessment ceiling and original deadline without resetting
commitments or attempts. Its separate database reads are a changing operational
snapshot. Scientific claim labels and real queue jobs are reported separately.

`builder-recovery-production-v1-status.json` is written only after deployed-source,
private RLS, active admission/polling, public desktop/mobile browser, volume and
assessment audits pass. It records remaining conditional checks. A fresh
authenticated upload, scope confirmation and chat are not reverified by the
isolated signed-out browser. Software readiness does not imply complete scientific
coverage or a guarantee of conclusive results.
